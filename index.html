Psybeam

Play Pokémon FireRed together. One player runs the real game; friends join their world as guests, walking it with them, talking to its people, and battling each other and the host.

Join a game

Open the invite link or QR code the host sent you: that's all. Your browser connects straight to their game.

New guests wait in the host's Waiting Room until they're let in, unless a friend's invite brought them.

Open the game

Host a game

Psybeam runs on your Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later) with your own copy of Pokémon FireRed (USA, Europe) (Rev 1).

  1. Download Psybeam, unzip it, and move it to Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS can't check it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Add your ROM on its Home tab, then share its invite link or QR code.

Keep it on your device

Once you've opened the game here, your browser keeps it: invite links and QR codes still open it if this site ever can't be reached. You can also install it as an app.

In your browser's menu, look for Install or Add to Home Screen.
